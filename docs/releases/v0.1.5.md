# AsySounds v0.1.5 — Voice-safe transient processing

## Fixed
- Prevents the impact detector from applying broadband whole-frame gain reduction while speech is active. The previous detector could cause voice pumping or buzzing in diagnostic tracks 4 and 5.
- Treats brief, isolated clicks as local events (~2 ms neighborhood) rather than muting the entire 10 ms frame or holding the gain into the next syllable.
- Strengthens attenuation for sparse clicks at moderate slider settings while leaving the rest of the voiced frame unchanged.
- Shortens whole-frame clap/reverb reduction between phrases and clears residual attenuation immediately when speech resumes.

## Testing
- Regression tests check clean sustained voice, click attenuation at 45%, unmodified samples away from each click, absence of persistent ducking after a clap, and isolated clap detection.
- Existing RNNoise, VAD, WAV diagnostics and virtual-cable routing remain unchanged.

## Important limitation
- **This release prioritizes intelligible speech over removing broad claps that overlap speech.** A lightweight transient detector cannot reliably isolate the two sources; those claps may remain audible until a dedicated noise-separation model is integrated and validated against actual microphone recordings.
- Breathing and keyboard/mouse ambience may still be audible. Voice clarity at 100% can accentuate some high-frequency sound.
- Real-microphone audio quality is not verified by passing unit tests. Please compare stage 2 (Neural only), stage 3 (VAD), stage 4 (Impact) and stage 5 (Final) using the same spoken phrase.

## Compatibility
- Windows x64 portable; WebView2 required. No changes to Windows default audio devices or Sonar.
- Optional VB-CABLE-compatible driver required for virtual microphone routing.
